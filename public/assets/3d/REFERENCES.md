## Representation limits

This is a reference-informed Phase 1 visualization, not a manufacturing-accurate digital twin. Reliable exterior scale anchors are preserved. Interior mounting coordinates, pump housing details, PCB details, fan blades, GPU facets and cable paths are simplified visual approximations. Case fan manufacturer and RM850e revision were not specified. No CPU SKU is claimed underneath the AM5 pump.

The SSD occupies an exposed lower M.2 bay to remain independently inspectable; the upper M.2 thermal guard is installed. Exact board clearances and the thick AIO's fit are visually checked, not certified for purchase or physical assembly.

## Optimization reference

[glTF Transform CLI documentation](https://gltf-transform.dev/cli) describes `dedup`, `weld`, `prune` and vertex reordering. The delivered asset already uses indexed geometry, shared fan and RAM meshes, shared PBR materials and no textures. No compression decoder is required.
